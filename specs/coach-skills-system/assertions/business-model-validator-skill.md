# Business Model Validator Skill

Coach has business model validation capability that conducts systematic startup/business assessment.

## Success Criteria

- Conducts structured assessment across 6 key areas:
  1. Industry background & expertise
  2. Product validation
  3. Market demand
  4. Business goals & strategy
  5. Traction & momentum
  6. Hypothesis prioritization

- Uses scoring methodology: 2 points (clear/evidence), 1 point (adequate), 0 points (unclear/concerning)
- Asks probing follow-up questions based on responses
- Tracks answers and identifies gaps throughout session

## Validation Framework

**Sample questions by area:**
- Industry: "What relevant experience does the founder have?"
- Product: "What stage of development? Any user testing?"
- Market: "What evidence exists for demand? Target customer?"
- Goals: "Primary goal? Monetization strategy?"
- Traction: "Current metrics? Funding raised?"
- Hypotheses: "Riskiest assumptions? What could invalidate this?"
